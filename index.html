Your tabs, pulled together.

A Chrome extension that gathers a site's tabs into one window.

Add to Chrome How it works

How it works

  • One click. Open any page from the site you want to tidy and click the toolbar icon. Every tab from that site, in every window, moves into one new window, so you can review them, keep what matters and close the rest.
  • Tidy everything at once. Right-click the icon and choose “Clean up all windows” to sort every window's tabs into one window, grouped by site.
  • Nothing is closed. Tabs are moved, not closed. An optional setting can also close exact duplicates.
  • Optional tab groups, named after the site.
  • Private. No account, no ads and no tracking. Tab addresses are read only on your device.
