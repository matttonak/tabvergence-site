Privacy policy

Tabvergence does not collect, transmit or share any data.

What the extension reads

To gather tabs by site, Tabvergence reads the addresses of your open tabs. This happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the extension makes no network requests.

What it stores

Your settings are saved with Chrome's built-in storage, which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in browsers. A running count of tabs helped is kept on your device only.

Sharing

We don't sell, share or use any data for anything other than the extension's single purpose of organizing your tabs.

Feedback form

The extension collects nothing. If you choose to send feedback through the form on this website, it is handled by Google Forms and I receive only what you type. Please don't include anything private.
